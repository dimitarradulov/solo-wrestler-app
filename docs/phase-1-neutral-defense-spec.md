# Freestyle Foundations Curriculum

Status: Implemented in issue #12. Replaces the shorter two-workout Phase 1 plan.

## Structure

Foundations contains six ordered Weeks, each with Workout A, B, and C, for 18 Workout Instances using three reusable Workout Templates. Three sessions per week is a suggested pace; no date or missed-session reset affects progression.

- **A — Movement and Entry Mechanics:** stance, footwork, level changes, double-leg entries, and returning to neutral defense.
- **B — Defense and Recovery:** down-block, sprawl, re-square to a safe neutral position, then a separate double-leg entry.
- **C — Connected Attacks:** connect a simple wrestling setup, double-leg entry, and controlled finish on a supported dummy.

All templates include movement, attack, and defense with different emphases. Neutral Defense ends in a safe neutral position. A later entry is separate offense; these workouts do not introduce counteroffense, boxing, or self-defense content.

## Session budget

Each template has five ordered practice blocks budgeted at 10 minutes of warm-up, 15 minutes of movement/defensive posture, 20 minutes of focused technique, 10 minutes of connected practice, and 5 minutes of cooldown. The timed Work portions are 10, 13, 18, 8, and 3 minutes. The four existing two-minute automatic Rest periods start the following block, so each prescription shows the block budget and the Rest allocation once. The complete Estimated Duration is 60 minutes; no elapsed-time completion gate is added.

The focused drills use slow, controlled practice with self-paced recovery inside the timed interval. The same prescriptions repeat across all six weeks; progression changes the focus, not the automatic volume.

## Progression Focus

- Weeks 1–2: Individual positions and slow mechanics.
- Weeks 3–4: Connected movements and entries.
- Weeks 5–6: Consistent sequences from movement.

## Technique Videos

The stance, double-leg, and down-block/sprawl videos link to demonstrations listed by USA Wrestling under its Olympic Styles Level 1 curriculum. The video pages could not be opened during implementation, so each Technique Video includes a Video Note identifying the USA Wrestling label and stating that visual verification remains outstanding. The double-leg video is attached only to entry drills; down-block/sprawl content explicitly stops at re-squaring and excludes the source's continuation into counteroffense.

Primary content references:

- [USA Wrestling Olympic Styles Level 1](https://www.usawmembership.com/usa_wrestling_core_curriculum/4): Freestyle Stance, Blast double leg, and Down block & sprawl.
- [USA Wrestling Freestyle Level 2](https://www.usawmembership.com/usa_wrestling_core_curriculum/5): head position, crowding, making contact, double-leg entries, and defense.

These references support the technical topics; the session budget and solo dummy prescriptions are product adaptations, not USA Wrestling-approved programming.

## Curriculum Revision

Adopting this replacement curriculum increments the stored Curriculum Revision and uses ADR 0004's one-time reset. Existing progress, completed history and notes, and unfinished training are removed. Onboarding acceptance and unrelated preferences remain. Existing users with training data see a dismissible explanation; a fresh installation does not. Persisting the new revision prevents future launches from deleting new training state.
